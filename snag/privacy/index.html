Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This policy explains how Snag handles information when you use the Snag app and related services.

Information we handle

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we handle the provider account identifier and, where supplied by the provider, your name and email address. We keep a session token so the app can keep you signed in.
  • Wishlist information. We handle the product names, links, stores, prices, price history, budgets, and preferences you add to Snag.
  • Snag Intelligence conversations. We store the messages and results in your chat history when you use this feature.
  • Subscription information. Apple or Google processes purchases. We receive subscription and entitlement status from RevenueCat; we do not receive your payment-card details.
  • Technical information. The app reads your device language and currency locally to choose defaults. We do not collect precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

We use information to provide the wishlist, budget, price-check, account, subscription, and chat features; keep the service secure; and respond to support requests.

Service providers

We use Convex to host service data, Apple and Google for optional sign-in, RevenueCat to manage subscription status, and Hugging Face to host an optional on-device model download. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services under their own privacy policies.

Snag Intelligence processes prompts on your device when the feature is available. We do not send prompts to an external AI inference service. If the optional model is downloaded, Hugging Face receives the standard request information required to deliver that file, such as your IP address.

Sharing and sales

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking. We share information only with the service providers above, when required by law, or to protect Snag and its users.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as needed to operate Snag, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. To request access, correction, or deletion of information, contact us at contact.devjeff@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Snag is not directed to children under 13. Do not use Snag if you are under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Snag changes. We will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact.devjeff@gmail.com.
