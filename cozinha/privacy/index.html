Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Cozinha de Origem (called Brazilian Cuisine in English-language stores) handles information when you use the app. You can use the app without an account, and most of it works without sharing anything with us.

Using the app without an account

If you do not sign in, your saved recipes, pantry, cooking progress and chosen serving sizes stay on your device. We do not receive them. Anything you write on the wall while signed out also stays on your device.

Information we handle when you sign in

  • Account information. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we handle the provider account identifier and, where the provider supplies them, your name, email address and profile picture. Apple lets you hide your email address and may not share your name. We keep a session token in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android) so the app can keep you signed in.
  • Kitchen data. We store the recipes you save, the ingredients you mark as in your pantry, and the cooking steps you mark as done, so they follow you across devices. The first time you sign in, anything already saved on your device is merged into your account and then removed from local storage.
  • Wall posts. If you post on "I Cooked", we store the name you type, the dish, the state, the text and the time. Wall posts are visible to other people who use the app. Do not put information in a post that you do not want others to read.
  • Purchase information. If you buy Cozinha de Origem Plus, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive your subscription or purchase status from RevenueCat; we never receive your payment card details. RevenueCat identifies you by your account identifier when you are signed in, or by an anonymous identifier when you are not.
  • Technical information. The app reads your device language locally to choose Portuguese or English. When it checks for app updates, the update service receives standard technical request information such as your IP address and the app and runtime version. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

We use information to provide sign-in, sync and the wall; to unlock and restore your Plus purchase; to keep the service secure and fix problems; and to answer support requests. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

Service providers

We rely on these providers, which process information as needed to run their part of the service and under their own privacy policies:

  • Convex hosts our database and backend functions.
  • Apple and Google provide optional sign-in and run the App Store and Google Play purchases.
  • RevenueCat manages purchase and subscription status.
  • Expo (EAS Update) delivers app updates.

Sharing and sales

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the providers above, when the law requires it, or to protect the app and its users. Wall posts are shared with other app users as described above.

Retention and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the account screen and choose Delete account. This permanently erases your saved recipes, pantry, progress and wall posts, your sign-in sessions and your account. It cannot be undone. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID or Google Play settings. Providers such as Apple, Google and RevenueCat may keep purchase records as their own policies and the law require.

If you cannot use the app to delete your account, email us from the address linked to your sign-in and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws, you may have the right to confirm that we handle your information, access it, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, and object to or limit some handling. Email contact.devjeff@gmail.com to exercise these rights. We may need to verify your identity first. Our basis for handling the information above is providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping it secure, and complying with the law.

International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours. We choose providers that protect information under their own policies and applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections and secure on-device storage for your session. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Cozinha de Origem is not directed to children under 13, and you must not create an account if you are under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the updated version here and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email contact.devjeff@gmail.com. This policy is also available in Portuguese; if the two differ, the Portuguese version prevails for users in Brazil.
